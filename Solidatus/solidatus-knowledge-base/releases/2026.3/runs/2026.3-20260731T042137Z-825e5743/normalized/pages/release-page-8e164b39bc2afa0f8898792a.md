# Data Domains interface

<figure><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Data Domains interface is a portal for navigating the structure and content of your Data Domains.

The Data Domains landing page gives you access to individual Data Domains that you created or have been shared with you. Once you open a domain, the Domain Spine keeps the main areas of the domain one click away, including Home, AI Assistant, Reference Hub, Lineage Models, saved Data Maps, and more.

From a domain homepage, you can search, open model and entry pages, review Analytics reports, and move into the Data Map without losing domain context.
