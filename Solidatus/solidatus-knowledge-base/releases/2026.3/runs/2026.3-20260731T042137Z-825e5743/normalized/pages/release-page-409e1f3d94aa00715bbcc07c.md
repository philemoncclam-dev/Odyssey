# Best practice main

Our model building best practice advice is designed to help you move from using Solidatus in the style of individual owners sharing individual models with other individuals to collaborative, efficient workflows that publish enterprise-wide, curated, and assured models for an entire organisation.

The best practices in this section emerge from real-world experience using Solidatus and establishing workflows in large, complex organisations. They help you avoid common pitfalls, work efficiently, and create models that deliver lasting business value.

**What you’ll find here**

The pages in this section provide guidance on three foundational aspects of effective model building:

* [Three-stage model building approach](/solidatus-best-practice/three-stage-model-building-approach)
* [Model topology](/solidatus-best-practice/model-topology)
* [Follow the thread and look left method](/solidatus-best-practice/follow-the-thread-and-look-left)

**The three-stage model building approach** provides a systematic three-phase method (Ingestion → Augmentation → Craft) for building models that satisfy a particular use case.

**Model topology** explains how to use multiple models to create both end-to-end lineage and also a curation and assurance workflow. This page explains types of models within a workflow topology: Connector forks, Atomic models, Join models, Composite models, WIP composite models, and Published models.

**The “look left” method** teaches both the discovery approach (working backwards from final targets of data – e.g. reports or consumed data products – through to source systems) and visual layout conventions (placing targets on the right, sources on the left) that make models intuitive to explore and understand.
