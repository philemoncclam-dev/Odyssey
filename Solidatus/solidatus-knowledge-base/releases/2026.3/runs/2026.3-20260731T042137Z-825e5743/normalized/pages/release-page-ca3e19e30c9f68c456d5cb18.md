# Analytics reports

Analytics Reports give you the ability to gain quantitative insight into the data landscapes captured in your Data Domains. You can gather key figures, build charts and data infographics, and share them across your organisation to improve monitoring, analysis, and decision-making.

<figure><figcaption><p>Create Analytics reports for your Data Domains</p></figcaption></figure>

Inside an Analytic Report, you define [metrics](/data-domains/analytics-reports/understand-analytics-reports-and-metrics#what-is-a-metric) that calculate the values you are interested in. You can then build different types of charts to visually analyse the metrics you’ve collected.

You can do a lot with Analytics Reports:

* Provide quantitative evidence of compliance with regulations
* Keep track of performance and governance targets
* Monitor quality, risk, or data governance maturity metrics
* Base your data and metadata management decision-making on numbers

What’s more, you can navigate seemlessly between analysis of a chart in an Analytics Report and examination of detailed information on domain entries represented by figures in the chart.
