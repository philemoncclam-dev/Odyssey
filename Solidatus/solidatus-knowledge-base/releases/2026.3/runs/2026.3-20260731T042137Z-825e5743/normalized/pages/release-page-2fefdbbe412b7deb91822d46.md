# Share and collaborate

Effective Data Management requires the co-operative efforts of a community of users, collaborating and enriching metadata and models. It is rare to find an organisation where a single person takes responsibility for the definition and management of metadata or models, so the ability to share responsibilities with others is essential. The ability to share content outside the metadata and modelling community is also essential.

Collaboration and ownership are core Solidatus concepts, giving subject matter experts across the organisation power to take ownership of the definition and maintenance of metadata within their respective areas of expertise.

Simple collaboration methods include sharing ownership of models, inviting other users to be authors, and sharing read-only links to models.

More advanced collaboration is supported by Solidatus [Activities](/models/share-and-collaborate/activities-and-activity-types).
