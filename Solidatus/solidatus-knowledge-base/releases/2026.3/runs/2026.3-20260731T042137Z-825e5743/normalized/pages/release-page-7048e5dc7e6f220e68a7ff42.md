# Share with Solidatus support

Sometimes you will need to share your full model, including display rules, with the Solidatus support team to resolve an issue.

1. Follow the instructions at [Transfer Models between Solidatus instances](/additional-resources/advanced-topics/transfer-models-between-instances) to export the model as a Solidatus JSON (or SOL) file.
2. Send the generated file to [support@solidatus.com](mailto:support%40solidatus.com).
