# Welcome to Solidatus

*Build and explore interactive blueprints of* ***data lineage** across your entire organisation.*

Our mission is to make fine-grained data lineage easy to capture, understand, and analyse, even for the largest, most complex and highly regulated organisations in the world.

Explore all aspects of Solidatus using the cards below or the sidebar on the left.

{% hint style="success" %}
If you're building your first models, you might want to start by [importing model content](/solidatus-2026.1/get-started/import-model-content) or using our [connectors](/solidatus-2026.1/connectors/connectors-overview) to extract lineage and metadata from external systems.
{% endhint %}

The team is always happy to receive feedback and help with your support queries.
