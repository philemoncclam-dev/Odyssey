# Solidatus 4.5

We are pleased to announce the general availability of Solidatus 4.5.

With release 4.5, Solidatus streamlines our collaboration capabilities by introducing the Practitioner licence, creating a new class of users that can view models like everyone else can, and participate in model development at the request of other users, via [Tasks](/models/share-and-collaborate/activities-and-activity-types/tasks). The creator of a Task can focus the efforts of a Practitioner, by identifying the individual entities they can modify (and possibly their descendants in the model hierarchy) and also by limiting the types of entities they can work on.

Practitioners can also contribute to discussions on models shared with them, including the Tasks they have been assigned to.

***

Restricting the scope of a Task is not just for Practitioners, it’s available for every Task, no matter who it’s assigned to. If you want to ask someone to review all the attributes in your model, with or without the ability to create new ones, now you can.

***

To help everyone keep track of their workload we’ve introduced the [My Activities](/models/share-and-collaborate/activities-and-activity-types/activities) page, where you can see the status of everything you’re involved with. The list of Activities looks the same as the Activities tab list in the [Model Overview](/the-user-interface/models-ui/model-overview), offering mostly the same functionality and allowing click-through to the detail page for an activity.

***

[Model tags](/the-user-interface/models-ui/model-tags) are a great way to categorise your own models - as well using your own private tags, you can now use tags that are shared across the organisation. Tags are created when they are first used, and can be reused. The tags used on models can be shared or personal - personal tags (highlighted by a padlock symbol) are only visible to the user that created them. The tags used on model revisions are limited to the model, as are the tags used on activities.

***

Lastly, but by no means least, we have introduced a new model browser to provide an improved experience when organising and finding models that are of interest to a user. It also provides more information about each model than the old Model Browser does, with direct access to relevant dialogues. If you prefer the old model browser don’t worry, it’s still available.

To switch between the old and new Model Browser, look out for these icons at the top of the list of models
