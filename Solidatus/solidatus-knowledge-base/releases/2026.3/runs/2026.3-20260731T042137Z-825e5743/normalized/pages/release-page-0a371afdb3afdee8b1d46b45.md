# About Solidatus

Solidatus provides the world’s leading data lineage solution for the largest and most demanding companies in the world. It allows unrivaled visibility of complete data landscapes, pipelines, and flows, so complex organisations know precisely what data they have, where it came from, who governs it, where it flows, and what is missing.

## Formal Features

Solidatus is a platform for building and exploring interactive **models** that visually map data lineage and store technical, business, and reference metadata.

At the formal level, Solidatus models are bi-temporal, logical, directed property graphs that allow users to define and visualise directional connections between hierarchically nested metadata **entities**. These hierarchical entities are nodes in a model that can be connected to one another by edges referred to as **transitions**.

Solidatus combines this formal, visual graph structure with the ability to add further metadata as properties or reference relationships that describe, catalogue, and document entities and transitions in a model.

In addition, models can be queried to retrieve entities based on fine-grained metadata and lineage characteristics.

Solidatus models are equipped with bi-temporal version control, meaning that states of a model are preserved and can be revisited, compared with other states, and recovered. With version control, you can use Solidatus models to plan future states and progress toward them, while comparing present and future to the past.

While Solidatus models can represent nearly anything, and connections between entities can be defined by the modeller, their formal features were designed specifically for building auditable, interactive, end-to-end blueprints of complex data lineage pathways and for connecting technical data architecture to regulatory, business, or other reference frameworks.

Models are powerful, flexible information resources and analytical tools that can be built manually, via importing in a variety of formats, and by extracting metadata and fine-grained lineage from external, third-party sources using Solidatus Integrations.

Finally, models form the foundation of Data Domains, which are curated libraries of metadata and enterprise-wide, end-to-end lineage that make the rich metadata and lineage stored in models easy to discover, analyse, and understand by business users.

## **Use-Cases and Business Solutions**

While Solidatus models are flexible and support a wide variety of use cases, they are principally designed to map, explore, and govern data lineage in complex organisations.

For example, models trace data as it enters an organisation, flows through systems and processes, and is used in analytics and reporting. Solidatus is commonly used to model system applications, ownership responsibilities, data quality, regulatory obligations, and lineage flowing into key reports.

Solidatus delivers business value across the following key areas:

> **Regulatory compliance:** Provides evidence of compliance with regulatory frameworks (such as Basel III) that require fine-grained lineage and transparency into sources of critical data elements.
>
> **Data governance:** Delivers a live, extensive, business-focused view of your data. Know who owns what, which systems depend on one another, what policies and terminology exist, and how they fit within your governance framework.
>
> **AI governance:** Ensures compliant and auditable use of AI, with full visibility into upstream inputs and downstream outputs. Build a foundation for trustworthy AI in large, complex organisations.
>
> **Change management:** Delivers comprehensive oversight of strategic programs and quantifies the impact of changes, such as cloud migration, AI implementation, and technology infrastructure upgrades.
>
> **Data products:** Maps all data products to track how each is built, including ownership, access rights, cost and revenue implications, and business impact of modifications.
>
> **Decision making and reporting:** Traces data used in decisions and reports to its origins, flows, and transformations, providing confidence in accuracy.
>
> **Operational resilience:** Maps system dependencies to reduce operational risk, minimize business impact, and accelerate system restoration during disaster recovery.
