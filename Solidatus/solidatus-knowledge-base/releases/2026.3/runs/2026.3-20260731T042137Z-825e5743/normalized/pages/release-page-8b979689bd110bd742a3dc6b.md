# Build Data Domains

Building a Data Domain involves two main steps:

1. **Prepare your models**: Set up Lineage, Reference, and Context models with the right content and relationships
2. **Create and publish**: Create the domain and publish your selected models to it

## **Recommended preparation**

However much planning you do up front, domains are most effective when they include:

* **Business context**: Reference models with meaningful terms and classifications
* **Clear relationships**: Connections between technical data and business concepts
* **Consistent metadata**: Well-documented entities with proper descriptions and properties
* **Cross-model integration**: Data Assets or composite models end-to-end lineage stitching

Visit [Model Architecture for Domains](/data-domains/understand-data-domains/prepare-models-to-publish-to-domains) to understand how model content surfaces in domains.
